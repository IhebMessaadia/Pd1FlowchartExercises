# PD1 Flowchart Exercises

Hand-drawn flowcharts and pseudocode from my first programming course (PD1, 2022). These are the exercises where I learned to design algorithms before writing any code.

## What's covered

- **Input and output**: reading numbers and printing results
- **Formulas**: sum of two numbers, circle area, Celsius ↔ Fahrenheit, feet → centimeters
- **Conditions**: even or odd, above/below/at freezing point, largest of three numbers, input validation
- **Loops**: counting, running sums and products, sequences between a low and a high value with a step
- **Digit manipulation**: reversing a number and checking for palindromes
- **Swapping and comparing** two values

Each exercise has the flowchart (Start/End terminals, input/output parallelograms, process boxes and decision diamonds), and many also have the step-by-step pseudocode.

The photos are in the repository root, in the order they were drawn.
